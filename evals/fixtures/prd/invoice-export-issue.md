# Issue 42: Invoice CSV export (label: stage:idea, accepted at triage)

Finance users at tenant organisations cannot get invoices out of the product. Today they ask support
for a manual query, which takes two days.

The export would include customer legal name, billing address, invoice number, line items and totals.
Tenants are in Canada and the UK. Exports could reach two million rows.

Accepted at weekly triage. Product owner: needs a PRD before design.
