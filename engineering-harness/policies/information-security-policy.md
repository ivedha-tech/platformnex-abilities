**Maps to:** SOC 2 CC1, CC2, CC5 · ISO 27001 clauses 5 to 7, A.5.1 to A.5.4

## Purpose and scope

Protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of company and
customer information. Applies to all staff, contractors, systems, and AI agents
acting on behalf of staff.

## Commitments

1. Security responsibilities are assigned to named people (see `docs/01-operating-model.md`).
2. Access is granted on least privilege and reviewed quarterly.
3. Every production change is reviewed, tested, and traceable to an approver.
4. Data is classified, kept in its approved region, and encrypted in transit and at rest.
5. Security events are logged centrally, retained, and monitored.
6. Incidents are reported without blame, handled to a defined process, and learned from.
7. Suppliers handling our data are assessed before use and annually.
8. Everyone completes security onboarding in their first week and a refresher each year. **(manual)**
9. Risks are recorded, scored, and reviewed quarterly.

## Device baseline (manual until MDM is in place)

Full-disk encryption, screen lock at 5 minutes or less, automatic OS updates,
a password manager, no shared accounts, company work in a separate OS or
browser profile on personal devices.

## Enforcement

Violations are handled through the normal HR process. Reporting your own
mistake promptly is always treated more favourably than concealing it.
