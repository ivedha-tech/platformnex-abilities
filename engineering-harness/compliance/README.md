# Compliance

- `control-matrix.csv`: 36 controls mapped to SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria and
  ISO/IEC 27001:2022, with the harness mechanism and the evidence each produces.
- `evidence-collection.md`: where evidence lives and the recurring tasks.
- `risk-register.csv`: starter register with 12 risks scored; assign owners.
- `vendor-register.csv`: starter vendor list; complete the blank columns.

Control references are a working map for your team, and your auditor has the
final say on how criteria are met.
