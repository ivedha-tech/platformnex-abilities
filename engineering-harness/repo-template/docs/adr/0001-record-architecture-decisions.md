# ADR-0001: Record architecture decisions

- Status: Accepted
- Date: __DATE__
- Deciders: tech lead

## Context
Decisions made in chat are lost, and auditors and new joiners both ask "why".

## Decision
We record significant decisions as ADRs in `docs/adr/`, numbered, one per
file, changed only by PR. Superseded ADRs stay and point to their replacement.

## Consequences
A small cost per decision. A durable record of design review (gate G2).

## Security, privacy, residency impact
None.
